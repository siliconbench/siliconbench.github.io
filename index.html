03 — Findings

What v1.0 tells us.

Full analysis →
Capability gap
84.0vs33.0

Proprietary agents lead by a wide margin.

Best proprietary vs best open-weights pass rate. When RTL and constraints must change together, Claude Opus 5.5 keeps 79.2%; GLM-5.3 falls to 4.2%.

Effort vs progress
2–3×

More steps, not better ones.

Open-weights agents use 122–136 median tool calls per task, versus 37.5–61.5 for proprietary agents, and spend about a third of them on unproductive loops.

Evaluation hacking
0/53

Agents tried to game the grader. None succeeded.

53 of 564 runs attempted a hack, up to 31.9% of runs for one open-weights model. Isolated Harbor grading blocked all 90 attempts.

06 — About

Chip designers check everything twice. Agents should too.

SiliconBench is built at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology by Prof. Zhiyao Xie's group, which works on AI for chip design.

Every task, grader and environment is open, so any result here can be reproduced or challenged. To have your agent evaluated, open an issue.
